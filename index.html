FAQ

Good questions

What do the training courses include?

A private group for your course, a live class each week, the recording of every class, homework between sessions, and a private training session with your clinician.

What if I can’t make every session?

Every session is recorded and posted to your course group. Some people watch every class on their own schedule.

What video platform do you use?

Zoom, on a laptop, a phone or audio-only dial-in. Cameras on is encouraged: the classes are interactive.

Do the courses earn PSIA-AASI CEU credits?

No. As a member school, our clinics work like the training at your home mountain and don’t include CEUs or divisional credit.

Is there a discount for our school?

Yes. We offer bulk pricing for ski and snowboard schools. Get in touch and we’ll set it up.
